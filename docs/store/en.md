## Full Description
Poswal Orbit is your calm, private, and distraction-free daily companion. Designed with a stunning liquid glass aesthetic and built strictly offline-first, Orbit combines your habits, tasks, and notes into one unified, peaceful orbit.

### 🌟 Why Poswal Orbit?
- **100% Offline & Private:** Zero tracking, zero telemetry, zero accounts. The app has no network permission—your data never leaves your device.
- **Calm Orbit Dashboard:** See your whole day at a single glance with our signature 4 AM day-boundary ring.
- **Smart Habits Engine:** Flexible daily, weekly, or interval schedules with streak tracking and encouragement—never guilt.
- **Subtasks & Priority Management:** Break down ambitious goals into manageable steps with urgent-to-low visual badges.
- **Markdown Notes & Checklists:** Capture quick thoughts, format headers and lists, and link notes directly to habits or tasks.
- **Interactive Home Screen Widgets:** Glance at your orbit progress, check off habits, and browse tasks straight from your Android Home Screen.
- **Safety Backups:** Automatic rolling 7-day local snapshots, JSON database export/import, and Loop Habit Tracker CSV compatibility.
